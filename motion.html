  • Colab free GPU = not guaranteed, 12h limit, setup 5–10 min.
  • Pollinations + Canvas = no quota, no queue, instant, works offline after page load.

What we do

  • LLM script: local instant or BYOK Groq/Gemini/OpenRouter/HF — hook + 3 facts + CTA, 18 words max per caption, 9:16 aware.
  • Images: Pollinations free CDN (no key) or your HF key; each scene gets prompt auto-built from caption + preset/palette.
  • Motion: Ken Burns + karaoke highlight + progress bar + badge, all Canvas — 30 fps capture via MediaRecorder → WebM (MP4 if browser).
  • Export: download video or the current frame as PNG, and copy the script for X Studio. Publishing and scheduling happen separately.
